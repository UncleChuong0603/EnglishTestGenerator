# Earned authority plan

Create assets people can recommend on their merits: the free Part 5 Challenge, the mixed Part 5 practice page, original mini exercises with explanations, and concise Vietnamese guides for common errors. Keep the landing URL stable and make it useful without a login wall.

Possible legitimate outreach, after an asset has been checked in production:

- University English clubs: offer a free practice link as an optional resource for a study session.
- Small TOEIC tutors: share a specific exercise or review method they can critique or assign.
- Study communities: answer a real learner question first, then link only when the page directly helps.
- Curated educational resource lists and relevant Vietnamese learning sites: suggest a resource that fills a genuine gap.

Record outreach recipient, relevance, asset, date and response. Respect each community's rules. Do not buy links, automate comments or profiles, mass-submit directories, fabricate testimonials, use link networks or trade links at scale. A link is a byproduct of a useful resource, never the success metric by itself.
