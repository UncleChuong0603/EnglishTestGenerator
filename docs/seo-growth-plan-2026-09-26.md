# TOEIC GYM: SEO growth review (2026-09-26)

## Current evidence and limits

- Search Console processed the submitted sitemap on 2026-09-25 and discovered 36 URLs. The owner supplied six **indexed-page examples**, but not the Page indexing summary filtered to this sitemap. Do not treat six examples as the total indexed count.
- The live SEO smoke check passed 36 sitemap URLs and seven application routes on 2026-09-26. The public guide pages have self canonicals, visible content, and internal links. The site is technically eligible for crawling; this does not predict ranking.
- The public SEO surface has an overview, a general online-practice guide, Reading Part 5–7 guides, one interactive Word Form page, and 20 published blog articles. It lacks dedicated public guides for Listening Part 1–4 and a public full-mock explanation page. The application has logged-in practice and mock routes; those routes should not be made indexable just to increase URL count.
- Search samples are observations, not a controlled Google Vietnam ranking study. Search Console query data and the filtered Page indexing reasons are needed before claiming a keyword opportunity or diagnosing non-indexing.

- [STUDY4's article index](https://study4.com/posts/) publishes topic guides alongside recent learner/test-experience articles. Its claimed outcomes and traffic are not verified here.
- These patterns show what those sites present to learners. They do not establish which factor caused their search positions.

## Recommendation: selective pages with a real practice outcome

| Priority | Deliverable | User value and acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pilot `/toeic/part-3` | Explain how to preview the three questions, follow a conversation, catch paraphrases, then let visitors try an owned Part 3 audio set with transcript and answer explanations. Publish only when the listening sample works without sign-in and the content is distinct from the existing Part 3–4 blog post. |
| 2 | Pilot one Part 5 subtopic, for example `/toeic/part-5/thi-dong-tu` | Reuse the Word Form pattern: a short explanation, five owned questions, explanations for correct and incorrect options, links to the parent Part 5 guide and the existing verb-tense article. Confirm enough reviewed questions are available first. |
| 3 | Expand to Listening Part 1, 2, and 4 guides | Give each Part a different listening task, worked example, and relevant practice entry point. Add links from the TOEIC overview, homepage, and related articles; include each only after the page is live and indexable. |
| 4 | Consider a public `/thi-thu-toeic-online` landing page | Describe actual mock format, access requirements, what results include, and an honest free entry point. Do not imply the full mock is free or guest-accessible if it requires an account or entitlement. |
| 5 | Add a factual editorial/about page | Identify the real people or organization responsible for lessons, how answers are reviewed, corrections/contact process, and any source/affiliation boundaries. Do not invent credentials or testimonials. |

Start with the first two pages and evaluate their indexing, impressions, and learner completion before producing a larger batch. New pages should satisfy a distinct search intent and learner task; publishing many near-duplicate keyword variants would make the site harder to use.

## Distribution and measurement

1. Publish a short, useful explanation or worked question in a relevant learner community or social channel, with a link to the matching exercise only when the community permits it. Seek genuine teacher or learner feedback and correct errors publicly. Avoid paid ranking links, bulk directory submissions, and automated comments; see [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).
2. For each new page, track its intended query group, publishing date, impressions, clicks, and indexing status in Search Console. Track the practical outcome separately: starts, completions, and whether learners continue to another lesson. With the present tiny Search Console sample, use changes over several weeks rather than daily rank checks.
3. Recheck Page indexing filtered by the submitted sitemap. Inspect representative URLs marked Discovered/Crawled but not indexed before changing content or technical settings. The six currently shown examples and 36 discovered sitemap URLs do not answer this question.

Google's [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) prioritizes useful, original content, clear links, and promotion, and says there is no automatic route to first place. Its [people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports accurate authorship and explaining how content was produced where useful. These are quality and discovery practices, not guaranteed ranking factors for this site.
