# TOEIC vocabulary study list (5,000 entries)

The app contains 5,000 unique study entries. The first 100 are original, hand-written topic entries. The next 900 are prioritized by the number of distinct question or passage texts containing a word in the local TOEIC GYM practice bank, with 55 additional workplace words selected to reach 900. This ordering measures this project's authored practice material, not actual TOEIC test frequency. ETS does not publish an official top-5,000 ranking; its [examinee handbook](https://www.eu.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/eu/pdfs/toeic/toeic-listening-reading-test.pdf) describes common settings and explicitly says specialized business vocabulary is not required.

The expansion was prepared from 750 Part 5 questions, 475 Reading sets, and 1,152 Listening items, plus local Listening expansions. Identical texts were counted once. Standalone wrong multiple-choice options and explanations were excluded from ranking; Listening transcripts can still contain spoken response options. Word variants were grouped when possible. Terms that were names, mainly grammatical function words, or misleading inflected forms were removed. Meanings for common ambiguous terms were corrected for workplace use. Of the 900 new entries, 647 have an example sentence found in the local practice content. Entries without a suitable example show the term and meanings without an invented sentence.

The expanded English and Vietnamese sense data is adapted from [thichhoc-dict](https://github.com/thichhoc-org/thichhoc-dict), commit `4d6e92e8bcf8e3e762410c2b0a9f98fea8e62e5b`, and is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The dictionary's [full data attribution](/public/vocabulary-data-attribution.txt) is included in the product and linked from the study page. Its source list includes WordNet 3.1, CMUdict, Wiktionary, and wordfreq. The dictionary describes itself as a beta with machine-generated senses, so individual meanings may need further editorial correction.

The final 4,000 terms come from the same dictionary revision. `scripts/generate-vocabulary-5000.mjs` reads a local checkout of that revision, keeps single English words with paired Vietnamese and English senses in frequency tiers 1–3 and a positive WordNet tag count, filters existing terms, inflected plurals, selected grammatical words, and common proper-name patterns, then orders the remaining terms by the dictionary's general English frequency score. Where a headword has several parts of speech, its WordNet tag count helps select the more common sense. Raw English glosses are not paired with unrelated Vietnamese translations. These sets are general English vocabulary for TOEIC study, not terms ranked by occurrence in the TOEIC GYM bank. They have no invented example sentences. The dictionary senses are machine-assisted and have not all received editorial review.

To regenerate: clone `https://github.com/thichhoc-org/thichhoc-dict`, check out commit `4d6e92e8bcf8e3e762410c2b0a9f98fea8e62e5b`, then run `node scripts/generate-vocabulary-5000.mjs <checkout-path>` from the app root. The first 1,000 entries retain their keys and ordering, so existing saved vocabulary cards keep working.
