## Long description

TOEIC GYM helps you practice TOEIC Listening & Reading by Part, understand mistakes and choose the next useful step from real learning evidence.

Key features:

- Today’s Workout based on progress and review needs.
- Focused Part 1–7 Listening and Reading practice.
- Mistake Bank with learner-selected reasons and targeted retry practice.
- Vocabulary review schedule with a limited offline review queue.
- Short Dictation sessions for detailed listening practice.
- Progress by Part, skill and evidence range; no TOEIC score prediction.
- Vietnamese and English interface and explanation preferences.
- Optional learning reminders with separate controls for each notification type.
- In-app data export and permanent account deletion.

TOEIC GYM is an independent practice platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ETS. Results are raw practice accuracy, not official TOEIC scores.

## Review notes draft

The app requires a verified account. Add a dedicated review account in App Store Connect/Play Console; never commit review credentials to Git.

After signing in, reviewers can:

1. Open Home to start Today’s Workout.
2. Open Practice to select Part 1–7.
3. Open Progress to review evidence by skill and Part.
4. Open Settings → Learning reminders to opt in to push.
5. Open Settings → Account & data to export JSON or delete the account.

The app does not show an external-payment call to action and does not claim a predicted TOEIC score.
